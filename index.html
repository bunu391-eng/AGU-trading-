    Common indicator logic

    • RSI above 70: potentially overbought; below 30: potentially oversold.
    • EMA alignment helps describe trend direction.
    • MACD relationship describes momentum direction.
    • ADX measures trend strength; it does not identify direction by itself.
    • ATR describes recent volatility and can help contextualize stop distance.

    Important limitation

    Technical indicators are derived from historical price data. They can conflict, lag price, and produce false signals. The dashboard deliberately labels its output as analysis rather than a guaranteed buy/sell signal.
